GAME RULES

Roll the dice and travel across revolutionary France. Collect coins, avoid the Angry Mob, and try to reach the Guillotine before everyone else.

Movement

Players normally roll two dice and move forward according to their combined result.

Coin Spaces

Landing on a coin space rewards the player with additional coins.

Angry Mob

Landing on an Angry Mob space means the player must use only one die on their next turn.

Think Spaces

Players may choose the safe route or risk the Dark Alley shortcut. Attempting the Dark Alley requires a coin flip. Heads means survival; tails means dishonorable death.

Reach the Guillotine

The first player to reach the final space wins!
